Our Story

Founded on a simple belief

Vertebra & Vitality opened its doors in 2011 with a single treatment room and a simple belief: the body has a remarkable capacity to heal when the spine and nervous system are properly aligned. What started as a small neighborhood practice has grown into a trusted multi-doctor clinic, without ever losing the personal, unhurried approach our first patients came to rely on.

Today, our team blends traditional chiropractic technique with modern posture diagnostics and rehabilitation science — treating everyone from weekend athletes to expecting mothers to desk-bound professionals dealing with chronic tension.

Our Journey

Fifteen years, one patient at a time

2011

The First Treatment Room

Dr. Whitfield opens a single-room practice in Southeast Portland with a simple mission: unhurried, evidence-based spinal care.

2014

Our Second Doctor Joins

Dr. Feld comes aboard to launch our sports injury program, partnering with local high school and club teams.

2017

A Larger Home

We move into our current Willowmere Ave location, tripling treatment space and adding an on-site rehab studio.

2020

Telehealth Consultations

Virtual posture consultations launch, helping patients get guidance safely from home during a difficult year.

2024

10,000th Patient Milestone

We welcome our 10,000th patient and are voted Portland's top-rated chiropractic clinic by local readers.
