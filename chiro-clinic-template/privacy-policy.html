Privacy Policy

How Vertebra & Vitality collects, uses, and protects your personal and health information.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request an appointment, create a patient account, or contact our clinic, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, insurance details, and information about your symptoms or condition.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type and pages visited, to help us improve this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to schedule and confirm appointments, communicate with you about your care, process insurance and billing, and respond to questions submitted through our contact forms.

We do not sell your personal or health information to third parties.

3. Protected Health Information

Any health information you share with our clinic is treated as confidential and is handled in accordance with applicable healthcare privacy regulations, including HIPAA where applicable. Access to your health records is limited to clinic staff directly involved in your care.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your theme preference and to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with your insurance provider for billing purposes, with other healthcare professionals directly involved in your treatment, or when required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information on file with our clinic at any time by contacting our front desk or emailing us at care@vertebravitality.com.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at care@vertebravitality.com or call +1 (555) 210-7744.
